HANDCRAFTED IN THE UK

Florentine-Style Wax Sachets

Each ASTER HOME sachet is poured, scented and decorated by hand, following the traditional concept of Florentine scented wax sachets.

Every piece is individually finished, so subtle variations in flowers, colour and placement make each sachet beautifully unique.

Decorative scented wax only — do not light or melt. Keep away from heat, direct sunlight, delicate surfaces and out of reach of children and pets.

A LITTLE SCENT, WHERE YOU LOVE IT

Where to use your sachets

A gentle way to bring fragrance to wardrobes, drawers, luggage and other suitable small spaces.

Wardrobe

Hang among clothes, away from direct contact with delicate fabrics.

Drawer

Place in a lined drawer or small box, avoiding direct contact with fabrics and surfaces.

Luggage

A pretty scented companion for stored luggage or a packed case.

Car — not recommended

Do not use in vehicles: parked cars can become hot enough to soften or melt wax.

Small spaces

Lovely for suitable shelves, cupboards and other small areas.

Helpful details

Frequently asked questions

Are these candles?

No. These are decorative scented wax sachets without a wick. Do not light, burn or melt them.

Can I choose my colours and decoration?

Yes, you can share preferences in your enquiry. Each piece is unique; requests are subject to available materials and safe decoration choices.

Can I pay online now?

Not yet. This is a preview, not a live store. Prices, payment and shipping will be announced after product and safety checks.

Are sachets safe around pets?

Keep wax sachets and loose decorations entirely out of reach of pets and children. Do not place where they can be chewed, swallowed or exposed to heat.
